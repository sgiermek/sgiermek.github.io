Privacy policy

homelab-rclone is used only by its owner, to store encrypted backups of the owner's home server in the owner's own Google Drive.

It accesses Google Drive solely for that purpose. No data from Google APIs is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and no data about any other user is collected.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
